Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

AlgoLadder is a free DSA progress tracker. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

If you use the app without signing in, your progress (completed problems, revisit marks and notes) is stored only in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account's email address and a unique user ID. We do not receive your Google password. We use this only to identify your account and to save your progress.

How it is used

Your progress data (problem IDs, completion status, revisit marks and notes) is stored in a database hosted by Supabase so it can sync across your devices. Access is restricted so that each user can read and write only their own data. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for tracking.

Third parties

Authentication and storage are provided by Supabase and Google Sign-In (Google Privacy Policy). The site is hosted on Netlify. Problem links point to LeetCode, which has its own policies.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You can delete your saved progress with the "Reset progress" button, which also removes it from our database. To have your account data deleted entirely, contact us at the address below.
